Questions we get asked most

Will the Future Lens Agent actually sound human?+
Yes. We use the most advanced conversational AI available and train it on your brand voice, your services, and your typical customer conversations. Most people do not realise they are talking to an AI agent until the call ends.
Do I need to replace my current CRM?+
No. Future Lens AI connects on top of whatever you already use including HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, Zoho, and even a spreadsheet. We enhance what you have, not replace it.
What does outbound reactivation actually mean?+
It means our agent picks up the phone and calls your old, cold, and forgotten leads. People who said not now, no shows, or leads that went quiet. It has a natural conversation, gauges interest, and if they are ready, transfers them live to your team or books them directly.
How quickly can you get this set up?+
Most businesses are fully live within 48 hours of their onboarding call. We handle everything including training the agent, connecting to your CRM, and setting up both inbound and outbound campaigns.
Is this only for large businesses?+
Not at all. Whether you are a solo operator or a team of 50, if you have leads in a CRM that are not being followed up properly we can help. The agent scales to your volume automatically.
What is included in the free consultation?+
We look at your current setup, identify where leads are leaking, estimate the revenue you are missing, and show you what we would build to fix it. No charge, no commitment, just a clear picture of the opportunity.
